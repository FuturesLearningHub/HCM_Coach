# Uploading Documents to the Document Records Module in Oracle Cloud
Job aid, last updated 2/19/25.

Purpose: upload documents (salary changes, reclassifications, bonuses, alternative work arrangements, performance reviews, disciplinary letters, resignations and so on) to Document Records in Oracle Cloud.
Requires Oracle Cloud access with permission to upload documents, and digital copies of the documents.

## Step 1: Open Document Records for the employee
Either:
1. From the Home page, search for the employee.
2. Click the drop-down arrow next to the employee's name and select "Document Records".

Or:
1. From the Home page, select the "Our Workforce" tab.
2. Click "Show More" under "Quick Actions" on the left-hand side.
3. Scroll to the "Employment" section and select "Document Records".
4. Search for the employee using the search bar or "Advanced Search".

## Step 2: Start the upload
1. In Document Records, click "Add".

## Step 3: Select the document type
A window asks for the type of document. Choose from the drop-down:
- Compensation Letters: bonus, interim pay, salary increase letters.
- Employee Agreements: alternative work arrangements, assignment extensions.
- Leave and Accommodation Documentation: letters indicating approved leave (FMLA, non-FMLA, Parental Leave, Administrative Leave).
- Other: archived personnel documents.
- Performance and Discipline: performance improvement plans, disciplinary notices.
- Performance Review: performance evaluations, self-assessments.
- Work: visa and sponsorship documentation.

## Step 4: Enter document details
1. Review the document type "Description" for any specific upload instructions.
2. Enter the document name in the required "Name" field.
3. Enter the date the document was issued or delivered (usually found in the document) in the required "Issued On" field.

## Step 5: Upload the document
1. Find the document on your computer.
2. Drag the file into the "Attachments" box, or click the drop-down arrow next to "Drag files here or click to add attachment" and click "Add File".
3. Make sure the correct file was uploaded.

## Step 6: Submit
1. Review the details and the selected document.
2. Click "Submit" at the top of the screen.

Note: some uploads require approval from PCB before they appear in Document Records. If so, an on-screen notification appears after you click "Submit", and a notification is sent to the HCM after PCB approves the upload.
